§4  WHAT final-dod DID NOT VERIFY, AND WHY
==============================================================================

- The upstream leaderboard timings quoted in 01-definition.md and 03-technique-recon.md
  are facts about a 32-core EPYC limited to 8 cores. They were never re-run here and
  cannot be: spec.md:35 forbids comparing them against this machine's clock, and this
  report does not.
- No JDK 21+ on this machine (Java 8 JRE, no javac), so upstream's Java reference was
  never executed; the output semantics in 01-definition.md are derived from its source
  and pinned by the byte-compare against code/gen's own reference, not by running it.
- A genuinely COLD-cache number does not exist in this study. 'purge' needs sudo and
  this loop is unattended, so every published storage state is 'uncached' (F_NOCACHE)
  or 'page-cached', and the word cold is never used for either.
- The residual between the published headline and the final-dod re-measure has no named
  mechanism. E-36 prices one contributor (the 1b correctness read this ticket inserted)
  and refuses to invent one for the rest; see 07-experiment-ledger.md E-35/E-36.
